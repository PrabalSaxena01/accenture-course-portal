### Q1. Combinatorics & Letter Permutations

*Quiz Set: Module 14 Key Concepts* | *Type: problem*

**Question Statement:**
```
In how many distinct ways can the letters of the word 'ACCENTURE' be arranged?
```

**Options:**
- 45,360
- 90,720
- 181,440
- 362,880

**✅ Correct Answer:** `90,720`

**💡 Explanation & Solution Walkthrough:**
Total letters = 9. Frequencies: A:1, C:2, E:2, N:1, T:1, U:1, R:1.
Formula = 9! / (2! * 2!) = 362,880 / 4 = 90,720 distinct arrangements.
