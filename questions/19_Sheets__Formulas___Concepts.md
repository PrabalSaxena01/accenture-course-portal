### Q1. Successive Discount Calculation

*Quiz Set: Module 19 Key Concepts* | *Type: problem*

**Question Statement:**
```
An item marked at Rs. 1,000 is sold with successive discounts of 20% and 10%. What is the net selling price?
```

**Options:**
- Rs. 700
- Rs. 720
- Rs. 750
- Rs. 780

**✅ Correct Answer:** `Rs. 720`

**💡 Explanation & Solution Walkthrough:**
Method 1: Formula = -20 - 10 + (20 * 10) / 100 = -30 + 2 = -28% net discount.
Selling Price = 1,000 * (1 - 0.28) = Rs. 720.
Method 2: 1,000 * 0.80 = 800; 800 * 0.90 = Rs. 720.
